Mission briefing

There are 8 vaults, one for each SAT criterion, then a final vault. Every answer is on your printed rubric. Keep it beside you and keep checking it.

FIND IT locate a word or requirement
CLIMB IT put the verbs in order, lowest band to highest
SPOT IT what changes between two bands
PLACE IT apply the rubric to real student work

Scoring: 5 points first try · 2 points second try · a hint costs 1 point. Ranked by points, then time. The clock starts when you press GO.
